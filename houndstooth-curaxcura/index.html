Who, Why and how

Cura × Cura is a modern Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) platform that bridges ancient healing wisdom with modern scientific innovation. Designed for practitioners, it simplifies how doctors prescribe and access Chinese medicine, enabling them to focus on patient care while Cura × Cura provides professional support.

They wanted their new brand identity to reflect their mission of making TCM more youthful, accessible, and aesthetically appealing.

We approached this project by honouring the depth of Traditional Chinese Medicine while reinterpreting it through a modern, functional lens, ensuring the brand feels culturally grounded, relevant, and intuitive for modern-day TCM practitioners.

Logo Inspirations

Traditional Chinese Architectural Shapes

Shooting Star Symbolising Transformation and Change

The architectural motifs nod to TCM as the foundational architecture of wellbeing, time-tested systems that structure and support vitality. The final Cura X Cura logo consolidates a shooting star-like form in an abstract ‘X’, representing a moment of alignment and release. In the context of Traditional Chinese Medicine, this reflects the instant when qi begins to flow freely, restoring balance after stagnation.

When combined with a traditional Chinese architectural motif, the shooting-star feels grounded rather than cosmic — suggesting breakthrough within structure, and progress that is rooted in tradition rather than detached from it.
